Launch on anything Robinhood Chain runs.

Dozens of launchpads. Almost none of them documented. This reads the chain, works out how each one is called, proves it by simulating a launch against the live contract, and gives you one way to drive all of them. Plus pools of your own, and a protocol that launches atomically with the liquidity locked.

Pick the venue, or be the venue.

The catalog

Nothing here was typed out.

Every ERC-20 that has ever existed emitted a transfer from the zero address when its supply was minted. Find those, take the transaction that produced each one, and the contract it called is by definition the thing that launched that token. Group them, and the venues sort themselves by how many coins they really launched.

One real launch per venue is decoded and kept as a template. A new launch reuses it and replaces only the parts whose meaning is known: the name, the symbol, the metadata, the salt. Everything else keeps the value that already worked on chain. Nothing is invented, defaulted, or zeroed.

Then every field is proved. Each one is substituted and re-simulated against the live contract, one at a time. What ships is not what inference guessed; it is what the venue accepted.

Why that last step exists

One venue's launch call carries two bytes32 arguments. Both look like salts. The first is a commitment to a launch configuration the venue publishes, and changing it reverts with LaunchEconomicsMismatch. The second is the real salt.

Nothing in the ABI, the argument order, or the values tells them apart. One eth_call each does, in about a second, and the catalog records which is which.

Library

Or drive it from code.

One dependency, viem. Dry run by default; live mode needs a deliberate out-of-band arm.

npm install launch-relay

npx launch-relay venues
npx launch-relay launch --venue virtuals \
  --name "Loop Rat" --symbol LOOPRAT

Relay every pump.fun graduation onto a venue of your choosing, or into a pool you own.

import { presets } from 'launch-relay';

const { relay } = await presets.pumpfunToPool({
  amm: 'uniswap-v3',
  poolType: 'single-sided',
  startFdv: 2,
});
relay.start();
Contracts

Proved against the chain, not against a mock.

Robinhood Chain serves eth_simulateV1, so the whole protocol is deployed and exercised on top of the current block on every test run: the real factory, the real position manager, the real fee tiers. Including the refusals, which are the part that matters.
